# SWOT Analysis (FODA)

| Strengths                                                                                                                                           | Weaknesses  |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------|
| Hybrid physical/digital platform<br>Modular technology<br>Strong local business connections<br><br>Impact: Enables scalability and differentiation. | Limited experience in digital payments<br>Moderate financial resources<br>Dependence on few riders<br><br>Impact: Requires training and progressive funding. |

| Opportunities                                                                                                                                        | Threats |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------|
| Growing demand for logistics<br>Partnerships with local banks<br>Sustainable expansion<br><br>Impact: Opens strategic alliances and regional growth. | Established competitors<br>Regulatory risks<br>Technological vulnerabilities<br><br>Impact: Requires reinforced security and compliance. |

**Impacto:**  
- Strengths → Scalability and differentiation.  
- Opportunities → Strategic Partnerships and Regional Growth.  
- Weaknesses → Need for training and progressive funding.  
- Threats → Strengthen security and regulatory compliance.
