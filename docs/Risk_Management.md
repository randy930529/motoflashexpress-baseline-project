# Risk Management

| Role               | Identified Risk                              | Mitigation Process |
|--------------------|----------------------------------------------|--------------------|
| Junior Developers  | Basic vulnerabilities in forms and payments  | 1. Apply encryption protocols following predefined guidelines.<br>2. Conduct simple unit tests.<br>3. Report vulnerabilities to senior developers for immediate correction. |
| Semi-Senior Devs   | Synchronization of orders and payments       | 1. Design more complex unit tests.<br>2. Simulate multiple order and payment scenarios.<br>3. Document results and adjust synchronization algorithms. |
| Senior Developers  | Security audits and system complexity        | 1. Lead quarterly security audits.<br>2. Define encryption standards and communication protocols.<br>3. Supervise incremental integration to reduce risk of massive failures. |
| Logistics Team     | Delays or duplicate orders                   | 1. Perform real-time simulations of rider assignments.<br>2. Validate that no duplicate orders exist.<br>3. Adjust assignment rules in the central system. |
| Project Managers   | Increasing complexity and traceability       | 1. Document each change in the risk matrix.<br>2. Apply contingency plans.<br>3. Ensure traceability between requirements, design, code, and tests. |
| QA Team            | Regression and security failures             | 1. Execute regression tests in each sprint.<br>2. Conduct security audits.<br>3. Validate compliance with requirements before releasing versions. |
| Legal Department   | Regulatory risks                             | 1. Review local regulations on transport and e-commerce.<br>2. Apply compliance checklist for each delivery.<br>3. Coordinate with project managers to ensure legal documentation. |
| HR Department      | Lack of specialists                          | 1. Assess required profiles.<br>2. Initiate recruitment processes. 3. Organize internal training to cover skill gaps. |
| Infrastructure Team| Technological overload                       | 1. Monitor server capacity.<br>2. Classify expansions as minor (new servers) or major (cloud migration).<br>3. Implement progressive scalability. |
